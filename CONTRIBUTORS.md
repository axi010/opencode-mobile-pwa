## How your name gets here

- **Translations** count (see `.i18n` tasks in the issues).
- **Screenshots / docs** count.
- **Bug reports that led to a fix** count — open the issue, and you get credited.
- **Reviews** count.

Credit is added in the same PR that merges your work, and your handle is also
mentioned in the release notes for that version.

If we ever forget you, open an issue and we will fix it immediately.
